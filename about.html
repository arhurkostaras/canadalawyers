About CanadaLawyers

Connecting clients with qualified lawyers across Canada through AI-powered matching and verified professional data.

Our Founder

The Person Behind the Platform

AK

Arthur Kostaras

Founder & CEO

Arthur Kostaras is the founder of CanadaLawyers. With a background in financial services and technology, Arthur built CanadaLawyers to solve a simple problem: finding the right lawyer shouldn't take weeks of research and networking. The platform uses AI matching and verified professional data to connect clients with qualified lawyers across Canada.

Why We Exist

The Problem We Solve

Finding qualified legal help in Canada is harder than it should be. We built CanadaLawyers to fix that.

Discovery Is Broken

Most people find a lawyer through word of mouth or a Google search that returns pages of ads. Neither approach surfaces who is actually the best fit for your situation.

AI Does the Matching

Our algorithm analyses 6 key factors — practice area, province, firm size, case type, language, and availability — to surface lawyers who are genuinely qualified for your needs.

Verified Data

Every lawyer in our directory is cross-referenced against provincial law society records. No scraped LinkedIn profiles, no stale data — real, active lawyers only.

Professional Independence

CanadaLawyers is a technology platform, not a referral mill. Every lawyer maintains full professional independence and all fee arrangements are between the lawyer and the client.
